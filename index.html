Research support

From search to evidence

A practical information-literacy pathway for medical learners and researchers.

01 · DEFINE

Frame the question

Clarify the topic, keywords, concepts and information need.

02 · SEARCH

Find quality evidence

Search catalogues, databases, journals and trusted scholarly sources.

03 · FILTER

Refine the results

Use filters, date, subject, study type and relevance to focus the set.

04 · EVALUATE

Check credibility

Assess authority, methodology, currency, relevance and evidence quality.

05 · ORGANIZE

Build your evidence set

Save useful articles, notes, citations and research leads systematically.

06 · CITE

Give credit

Use an appropriate citation style and keep source details complete.

Know your library

Smart study choices

Match the resource to the learning task.

📚

Textbook → Concepts

Start with the core text to establish a reliable conceptual foundation.

📰

Journal → Evidence

Use current scholarly literature to update your understanding.

📝

Question Paper → Revision

Use previous questions to identify patterns and strengthen revision.

🔬

Reference → Depth

Move to reference sources when you need deeper or comparative understanding.

💡

Lecture + Practical

Connect classroom teaching with practical learning and library resources.

🌐

Digital Source → Access

Use institutional digital resources for convenient off-campus scholarly access.
